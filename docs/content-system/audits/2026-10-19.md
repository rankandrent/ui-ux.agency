## Proposals (ranked by impact)

1. **Owner decision: Denver keyword overlap with the sister site.** uiuxdesignservices.us published `/location/ux-design-agency-denver/` on October 18, 2026 ("UX Agency Denver | SaaS, Aerospace, Outdoor & Energy UX"), targeting the same keyword as our `/location/ux-agency-denver/` (published October 16). 5-gram overlap is only 0.5%, but RULES ("never compete for the same local keywords") is now breached from the sister's side, and `sister-site.json` lists Denver, so a new Denver page here would be BLOCKED. Options for the owner: (a) keep ours (published first, AI/SaaS angle) and have the sister retarget a different variant such as "ux design agency denver"; (b) keep theirs and noindex or 301 ours; (c) keep both and split variants strictly ("ux agency denver" here, "ux design agency denver" there). I changed neither page.
2. **Get performance data flowing.** Without `docs/data/gsc/latest.json` or Semrush units, steps 4–5 of this audit and the learning loop can't run. Export GSC (last 28 days, query + page) weekly to `docs/data/gsc/latest.json` plus `summary-<date>.json`, or top up Semrush API units.
3. **Broken source on `/blog/saas-onboarding/` (#activation section).** `https://userpilot.com/blog/user-activation-rate-benchmark-report-2024/` returns 404, and its WordPress post ID returns 401 (unpublished). The sentence "averages 37.5% across 62 B2B companies" now cites a dead page. The fact-checker should find a live primary source (an archived copy or a current Userpilot report) or drop the figure. I didn't change it because I couldn't verify a replacement.
4. **City pages have no informational sources.** Add a short "Where we work remotely" line to `/about/` or `/team/` that links the three city pages (and future ones) with descriptive anchors, e.g. "AI and SaaS product design for Denver teams". This also fixes `/about/` (0 in-body inbound links), but it needs owner approval of the copy.
5. **Owner decision: home.ts "WCAG/AAA Achieved" badge** (`content/home.ts:227`). I found no audit, VPAT or source that supports AAA conformance, and the site's own rule frame is WCAG 2.2 AA. As written it's an unsupported claim (Honesty rule). Suggest removing it or replacing it with a verifiable statement ("Designed to WCAG 2.2 AA").
6. **Blog MOFU CTA picks the first related case study** (`app/blog/[slug]/page.tsx:107`, `relatedCaseStudies` in `lib/content.ts:240`). So Digno's "Overall revenue up 5x" shows on `/blog/how-to-design-an-mvp/` and `/blog/saas-homepage-design/`, where revenue isn't the topic. Suggest an optional `ctaCase:` frontmatter field (e.g. Vocable or Elia Wellness for the MVP post, TradeZella for the homepage post), or rank cases by shared service with `services[0]` first and a topic match next.
7. **De-template the closing H2s** "Have an AI or product to design?" and "Have an AI or SaaS to design?" (4 pages each). Vary the wording per page or make it a non-heading CTA, so repeated H2s don't dilute the heading signal.
8. **Homepage keyword.** `npm run onpage` expects "ui ux design services" on `/`, but the homepage leads with "UI UX design agency". The owner should pick one primary keyword for `/` and align title, H1, description and the first meta keyword, or fix the scorer's expectation.
9. **Strengthen `/blog/product-discovery/` instead of adding a "product discovery process" post.** Add an H2 "What are the product discovery phases?", targeting "product discovery phase" (170, KD 6) and "product discovery process" from blog-opportunities.
10. **Re-check queue:** CFPB 1033 NPRM publication, HTI-5 final rule, Colorado AG final rules (after October 26) and the xAI PI motion. Each changes one sentence on fintech, healthcare or Denver.
11. **`fingerprints.json` has 7 entries for posts that don't exist** (choose-ux-research-agency, saas-product-redesign, ux-audit-agency-vs-freelancer, design-system-roi, web-app-design-rfp, is-discovery-phase-worth-it, mobile-app-design-agency-vs-freelancer). Variety checks against "last 3 site-wide" may compare against ghosts. The pipeline owner should prune them or mark them `removed: true`.

## Pattern repetition across the site (with the rule that breaks it)

- **All 5 live posts are `type: Guide`, each opens with a definition H2, and each has exactly 6 visuals.** Rule: the next post in any silo must use a type other than the last 3 site-wide (Comparison, Checklist or Article), and its first H2 must not be a definition (lead with a decision, a cost or a comparison).
- **12 meta descriptions (4 of 5 posts, all case studies) end with "Book a free consultation."** Rule: end a description with the page's own payoff (a number, deliverable or outcome). Use a consultation CTA in at most 1 in 3 descriptions, and never on TOFU posts.
- **Location and industry pages link mostly to services and almost never to each other.** Rule: every city page's sector cards link the matching industry page once (as done for Minneapolis and San Diego).

## Learning loop

No ranking, click or lead data exists in `docs/` this week (no GSC export, no Semrush units, no lead-source export), so I recorded no performance pattern. Lessons for other agents, for their owners to add (I didn't edit their files):

- **content-link-builder:** when a post cites a case study (TradeZella in saas-onboarding, Nitro in product-discovery), link back from the matching problem sentence in that case study on publish day.
- **fact-checker:** re-check vendor-hosted benchmark reports (Userpilot) monthly. They get unpublished without redirects.
- **seo-keyword-researcher:** the opportunity list mixes service-adjacent topics with website-cost and tool-demo rows. Filter those before scoring, or weight `ui-ux-design-cost` toward product, app and SaaS cost.
