## About the Anyday App

The Anyday App is a mobile wallet and payout platform from XTM Inc., a fintech company (see our [fintech app design](/industries/fintech/) work) with offices in Miami and Toronto. It targets payroll pain in service businesses like hospitality and personal care.

Bi-weekly pay leaves hourly staff short between paychecks, so Anyday lets them reach wages they have already earned right away. The concept was sound, yet the first release felt clumsy to use. XTM brought us in to turn it into a product that employees enjoy and that makes payroll lighter for employers.

**What we delivered:** product revamp, strategy, web app, mobile app and website.

## Why workers and employers were frustrated

Service staff earn tips and shift pay daily, yet they often had to wait days to touch that money, and the old app did little to fix it.

- **Slow payroll:** standard payroll runs and cash workarounds kept workers waiting and cost businesses time.
- **A confusing first version:** the original Anyday experience was hard to navigate, so people used it less.
- **A promise not yet kept:** without a clear flow, the product could not solve the payroll problem it was built for.

## How we rebuilt the experience

We made the app easier and safer to use by adding real-time transfers and simplifying navigation across every screen. This [SaaS product redesign](/services/saas-products/saas-product-redesign/) served two audiences at once: employees who want their pay now, and employers who want less payroll hassle. Five experiences carry the product.

### Payments that land instantly

Every transaction shows up the moment it happens, so employees trust that their money is moving safely and quickly.

![The Anyday App list of recent transactions with payments to Amazon, Netflix, Spotify and Facebook](/work/anyday-app/recent-transactions.webp "Anyday App's recent transactions list: payments show up the moment they happen")

### Loading the wallet

Employees add funds to the wallet and check their balance in one glance, which keeps access to their money fast and protected.

![Anyday App cards with a $342.62 wallet load deposit and a $894.90 account balance](/work/anyday-app/wallet-load.webp "Anyday App wallet load and account balance cards for fast access to funds")

### A dashboard for business owners

Employers see their reward program, transaction details and financial metrics on a single screen, so decisions rest on real numbers.

![The Anyday App business dashboard with reward program stats, financial metrics and a transaction details bar chart](/work/anyday-app/business-dashboard.webp "Anyday App business dashboard: reward program, financial metrics and transaction details for employers")

### Spending by category

Users choose favorite categories, for example food, Walmart or health. That keeps spending tidy and surfaces offers tied to each one.

![The Anyday App Favorite Category screen where users choose 3 categories from options like Food, Health and Walmart](/work/anyday-app/favourite-category.webp "Anyday App Favorite Category screen for organizing spending by category")

### Cashback rewards

A rewards card tracks how close a user is to the next bonus, and one tap claims it.

![Anyday App cashback card: $10.00 bonus, progress reading $10 of $15, and a button to redeem](/work/anyday-app/rewards.webp "Anyday App cashback card tracking progress to the next bonus")

## What changed for employees and businesses

Employees now receive their earnings the instant they want them, which puts them in charge of their own cash flow. For businesses, the cleaner interface trims cash handling and day-to-day payroll friction.

Both sides gain confidence in their finances, and operations run leaner. That balance of user needs and business goals is what a [product design agency](/services/startups/product-design-agency/) should deliver.

In the mobile app, employees move from the Anyday card to their job list and back to a balance that sits above recent activity.
