## About Parents Connect

We designed the web and mobile community platform for Parents Connect, a non-profit that supports parents around the world. It exists so parents get the education, support and resources their kids need to do well.

Our job was to turn that mission into one product where parents meet, swap what they know and grow a community that spans countries.

**What we covered:** discovery and stakeholder interviews, a product revamp, UX design, plus brand and visual design.

## The problem we had to solve

The core problem was serving parents with very different backgrounds on one platform. To build a worldwide community, the product had three jobs:

- **Close the digital gap.** Confident and hesitant internet users both had to feel at home.
- **Keep people coming back.** A community only works when parents return, post and reply.
- **Work for everyone.** Country, language and device could not limit who joins.

## How we designed it

We began with research on what parents want from an online community. Those findings shaped the discussion forums, the learning and inspiration content, and the spaces where parents talk, organized into five core experiences.

### Video episodes with experts and real families

Parents watch short episodes that pair expert guidance with stories from other families.

### Parents from every country

A parent in one city can discover and follow parents in any other.

### Real-time chat for support

We added live messaging so parents can help each other in private threads or in groups.

![Parents Connect desktop chat screen with a conversation list, messages and a member profile panel](/work/parent-connect/chat-desktop.webp "Parents Connect desktop chat for real-time support between parents")

### Articles written by parenting experts

Experts post articles, and parents read them, react and talk them through.

Reactions and comment threads make each article or webinar a discussion.

## What we delivered

As a [product design agency](/services/startups/product-design-agency/), we delivered a full community product for web and mobile with:

- navigation that new users understand without help
- content suggestions tailored to each parent
- forums where parents connect and ask for advice
- parenting content curated by experts
- a worldwide events calendar that gives members a reason to return

The community feed shows posts, who is online, the members directory and chats in a single screen.

The members directory lets a parent find others close by, filtered by distance or interest.
