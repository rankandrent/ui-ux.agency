## About Nitro League

Nitro League is a decentralized racing game in the play-to-earn (P2E) model, driven by the Nitro token and NFTs that players can upgrade. One product had to win over gamers, NFT collectors and investors at once.

Several experiences live inside it:

- a 3D garage where players work together as a community
- simulation racing
- a metaverse for trading properties, production factories and garages
- an NFT marketplace

**Scope of work:** conceptualization and the journey from idea to product; art direction; sketching and modeling; an MVP launch plan; website and game design; and enhancements after launch.

![Nitro League brand assets: the logo, the N monogram, arcade art and racing gear](/work/nitro/brand-identity.webp "Nitro League brand identity from the branding workshops: logo, N monogram, arcade art and racing gear")

## Why the launch was hard

Nitro League launched into a market that did not yet trust crypto. When the team reached out to us, crypto and blockchain were still new ideas for most people. Investors questioned whether crypto products were real, and whether they would see any return.

Meanwhile, gamers and NFT collectors were arriving fast. The game had to win credibility and stand apart in a market that shifted month to month.

## What we designed

We shaped Nitro League around the people who would play, collect and invest, from the first concept all the way to go-to-market. Four experiences came out of that work.

### Simulation racing in 2D

We built the main racing loop as a 2D simulation, so new players understand it from their first race.

![Nitro League arcade mode art with a blue car drifting through streaks of light](/work/nitro/arcade-mode.webp "Nitro League arcade mode art from the 2D simulation racing game")

### A 3D garage players can explore

The garage is where players check their cars, upgrade them and show them off. It also works as the community's meeting point.

![A Nitro League 3D garage concept with a red car on an assembly platform beneath robot arms](/work/nitro/3d-garage.webp "Nitro League 3D garage concept art with a car on an assembly platform")

### Cars built to be collected

Every car got enough detail to feel like a collectible.

![The Horus Quake, a blue race car designed for Nitro League](/work/nitro/horus-quake-car.webp "The Horus Quake race car designed as a Nitro League collectible")

![The Nitro League garage on mobile, with the Sigma car, its top speed and the full cars collection](/work/nitro/mobile-garage-ui.webp "Nitro League mobile garage with the Sigma car and the full cars collection")

### A website that leads into the NFT marketplace

The marketing site covers the game, roadmap, whitepaper and tokenomics, then sends players and investors to the marketplace.

![The Nitro League website hero reading "A Futuristic Multiplayer Racing Game" beside a blue race car](/work/nitro/marketing-website.webp "Nitro League marketing website hero for the futuristic multiplayer racing game")

## How we got there

We opened with product discovery and interviews with stakeholders, as every [MVP design agency](/services/startups/mvp-design-agency/) project of ours begins. Those sessions gave us the user flows and the material for a detailed whitepaper.

Branding workshops then produced a bold identity for the brand. CG artists brought the gameplay to life, and design thinking guided the garage and website. Professional gamers tested and approved the final product.

![Nitro League NFT character art showing a figure holding a glowing orb of energy](/work/nitro/nft-character.webp "Nitro League NFT character artwork")

## What Nitro League achieved

After launching on a platform with a strong visual identity, Nitro League raised **$5M in funding in just 3 months**:

- **Funding:** launching on this platform attracted investors.
- **Awareness:** well-timed NFT sales got people talking about the game.
- **Upgradeable NFTs:** each NFT shipped with AR/VR and 3D visuals.
