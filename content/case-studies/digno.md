## About Digno

Digno is an HR tech SaaS that helps organizations run tasks, goals and rewards in an agile way. An AI scoring engine rates each employee on finished tasks, consistency and engagement, then sorts the result into four bands, with Poor at the bottom and Pro at the top.

Down the road, the same score may also support human resource pooling.

**What we delivered:** idea to product, strategy and design, branding, marketing content, web app and website.

## The problem leaders faced

Leaders were juggling many tools and still had no single picture of their teams and projects. Digno set out to build a product that would:

- **Show resources and projects in one place.**
- **Fit the goal frameworks teams already run**, whether that is OKRs (objectives and key results), KPIs (key performance indicators) or MBO (management by objectives).
- **Express performance as one plain score** that makes sense to managers and staff alike.

## How we shaped the product

We started with research: we collected requirements from stakeholders and end users to pin down the scoring formula, and we reviewed competitors that had attacked the same problem. ## What we built

The finished product is a central hub that stores employee data and computes the Digno score across three levels.

- **Organization score:** a single health score for the company.
- **Department scoring:** productivity, engagement, tenure and consistency for each department.
- **Individual employee scoring:** every person's score, tasks and progress.

Making an AI-generated score feel fair and readable is the kind of challenge we take on as an [AI UX design agency](/services/ai-products/ai-ux-design-agency/).

The organization dashboard puts the health score, leaderboard, department charts, employees and departments on one screen.

![Company-level view in Digno: summary tiles, a leaderboard, a radar chart for health and a bar chart per department](/work/digno/org-dashboard.webp "Digno company-level dashboard: health score, leaderboard and department charts")

Each department page splits its score into factors and sums up goals assigned, completed and pending.

![Development Department in Digno: health at 77.2, a percentage per factor and a summary of goals](/work/digno/department-score.webp "Digno department score: health, factor percentages and a summary of goals")
